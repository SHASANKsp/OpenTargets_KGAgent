Orientation

Everything here is evidence linking a target to a disease

Open Targets is organised around one central assertion — a target (gene/protein) is associated with a disease — and everything else in the release exists to support, score, quantify, or contextualise that assertion. That makes the data a natural fit for a property graph. Twenty evidence_* datasets each contribute a differently-flavoured Target → Disease edge (genetic, somatic, animal-model, expression, pathway, literature, clinical). The association_* datasets are the pre-computed roll-ups of those edges into a single harmonised score. Around this spine sit the supporting entities — drugs, variants, genetic studies, credible sets, tissues, pathways, phenotypes and publications — each connected by its own typed relationship.

The design in one sentence

Two hub node types (Target, Disease) carry a scored association backbone; a drug axis attaches therapeutics, trials and safety; a genetics axis (Variant → Credible Set → Study, plus L2G and colocalisation) explains why a locus implicates a gene; and a set of ontology / functional nodes (GO, Reactome, HPO, mouse phenotypes, tissues) give every entity biological grounding and hierarchy.

Worked traversal

From a GWAS hit to a therapeutic target

The single most valuable path in the graph resolves a non-coding association signal to the gene it acts through — the question that the credible_set, l2g_prediction and colocalisation datasets exist to answer. It is why the genetics axis is worth materialising in full.

What the graph is for

Applications extractable from the KG alone

Every application below is a graph query or a graph-ML task over the nodes and edges above — no external data required. Each card shows the entities traversed and the datasets that power it.

A

Target discovery & validation

Genetically-supported target selection

Rank targets for a disease by human genetic evidence, tracing each association back to a fine-mapped locus and a causal-gene prediction.

Disease ←assoc_with─ Target ←l2g─ CredibleSet ←from_study─ Study
Uses: association_overall, l2g_prediction, credible_set, evidence_gwas_credible_sets

Target safety & tractability triage

Filter candidate targets by developability — modality tractability, genetic constraint, known safety liabilities and essentiality — before committing.

Target · {tractability, constraint, safetyLiabilities, essential_in}
Uses: target, target_prioritisation, target_essentiality

Cross-species evidence corroboration

Confirm a human target–disease hypothesis with a matching knockout-mouse phenotype via the IMPC bridge.

Target ─model_phenotype→ MousePhenotype ≈ Disease/HPO
Uses: mouse_phenotype, evidence_impc, disease_phenotype

Novel-association discovery (link prediction)

Train KG embeddings on the association backbone to predict Target–Disease edges that don't yet exist — de-novo hypotheses ranked by plausibility.

embed(all edges) ⇒ score(Target ?→ Disease)
Uses: whole graph; supervised on association_overall
B

Drug discovery & repurposing

Drug repurposing by shared mechanism

Find drugs whose target is associated with a disease they are not yet indicated for — the classic two-hop repurposing query.

Drug ─targets→ Target ─assoc_with→ Disease ⟂ indicated_for
Uses: drug_mechanism_of_action, association_overall, clinical_indication

Polypharmacology & MoA mapping

Map a drug's full target set onto shared pathways and PPI neighbourhoods to anticipate on- and off-mechanism effects.

Drug ─targets→ Target ─{participates_in, interacts_with}→ …
Uses: drug_mechanism_of_action, target.pathways, interaction

Clinical & competitive landscape

Assemble the trial landscape for a target or disease — who is in which phase, and why trials stopped.

Target ─…→ Drug ─studied_in→ ClinicalReport →(status, whyStopped)
Uses: clinical_target, clinical_report, clinical_indication

Biomarker → drug-response linking

Connect a genomic biomarker to the therapy whose response it predicts, for stratified indication design.

Biomarker(Variant/Target) ─→ drugResponse ─→ Drug
Uses: evidence_cancer_biomarkers, evidence_intogen
C

Safety & pharmacovigilance

On-target adverse-event attribution

Test whether a drug's FAERS safety signal is shared across other drugs hitting the same target — separating on-target from off-target liability.

AdverseEvent ←has_adr─ Drug ─targets→ Target ←targets─ Drug'
Uses: openfda…adverse_reactions, drug_mechanism_of_action, drug_warning

Pharmacogenomic dosing rules

Surface variant–drug response relationships for a gene panel to drive precision-dosing or exclusion logic.

Variant ─pgx→ Drug · {genotype, directionality, evidenceLevel}
Uses: pharmacogenomics
D

Network & systems medicine

Network propagation for indirect targets

Propagate disease association scores across the PPI graph to nominate network-proximal targets that lack direct evidence.

seed(assoc Targets) → diffuse over interacts_with → rank
Uses: interaction, interaction_evidence, association_overall

Disease similarity & basket design

Cluster diseases by shared associated targets and ontology distance to find repositioning neighbours and basket-trial candidates.

Disease ─assoc_with→ Target ←assoc_with─ Disease' + subclass_of
Uses: association_overall, disease (ontology), disease_phenotype
E

Functional & regulatory genomics

Non-coding variant-to-function

Interpret a regulatory variant by the enhancer it sits in and the gene that enhancer controls, in the relevant tissue.

Variant ∈ Enhancer ─regulates→ Target (in Biosample)
Uses: enhancer_to_gene, variant, biosample

Tissue-restricted target maps

Require a candidate to be expressed in the disease-relevant tissue and absent from safety-critical ones — expression as a hard filter.

Target ─expressed_in→ Biosample · {RNA, protein level}
Uses: expression, evidence_expression_atlas, biosample
F

Literature & evidence intelligence

Literature-based hypothesis generation

Mine co-mention structure and literature embeddings for Target–Disease pairs that are emerging in text but not yet in curated evidence.

Publication ─mentions→ {Target, Disease} + vector(word)
Uses: literature, literature_vector, evidence_europepmc

Evidence provenance & novelty tracking

For any association, decompose the score by datasource and watch its novelty time-series to spot rising or decaying evidence.

assoc_with · {aggregationValue, timeseries, currentNovelty}
Uses: association_by_datasource, association_by_datatype
